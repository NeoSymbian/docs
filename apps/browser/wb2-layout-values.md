Part of [Apps](../README.md) · Status: reference · Updated: 2026-11-18 · Neighbours: [WB2](wb2-std.md), [the browser hub](README.md)

[wb2-std.md](wb2-std.md#all-layout-values) on 2026-11-27; the summary of what they mean is there, under
[Key facts for the `libc` port](wb2-std.md#key-facts-for-the-libc-port).
